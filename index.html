Other Notable Programming

Bookstore Crawl

Every October, the Wisconsin Union Directorate Literary Arts Committee spends one Saturday morning traveling through Madison to show committee members the local book stores where we source our literature for book drops in the Union’s Little Free Libraries.

The Annual People’s Farm Harvest Festival

Another October annual event that I assisted in the planning (and marketing) of is the People’s Farm Harvest Fest 2025! This event consisted of catered food, free arts and craft activities, live music, and so much more.
